# K9 Ventures

> K9 Ventures is a technology-focused Pre-Seed venture capital fund based in Palo Alto, California, founded by Manu Kumar. It provides the first institutional capital to companies at "frighteningly early" stages, backing new core technologies, technology platforms, and new markets.
